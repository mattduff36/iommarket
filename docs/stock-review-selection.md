# Stock review selections and received drafts

The review results use one open detail at a time. Landscape screens at least 1024px wide show a right-hand detail panel; narrower and portrait screens expand under the chosen result. Existing report links, browser history and selection across viewport changes are retained. Processing jobs also expand, including failed jobs without a saved report.

Review actions use original plan indexes and an exact saved fingerprint. Approve selected/all eligible queues only additions and updates; blocked, sold and removal suggestions cannot slip into approval. Reject clears review suggestions without deleting listings. Unselected items remain visible read-only while an approved batch processes, become selectable after success, and cannot be archived accidentally after a failure. A fresh stock check recovers a failed batch. Fully applied reviews retain their applied vehicle evidence.

Sending to drafts creates new DRAFT listings or DRAFT revisions of existing live listings. Live content is unchanged. Source ownership, protected conflicts, active jobs, allowance, stale data and duplicate requests are checked in a serializable transaction. A batch can contain up to 100 draft transfers; approve/reject supports the 2,000-row report limit. The existing draft list includes revisions of live listings.

Owned uploaded images are preserved and managed import receipts claimed atomically. Remote source URLs remain evidence, not uploaded photos: where no owned images are available the dealer must upload at least two valid photos. Source warnings are displayed in the editor. Submission checks stored details, required attributes, active category/location, owned photos and explicit acknowledgement of the exact received draft, including the featured submission path. Dealer deletion requires confirmation and an ownership/lifecycle check; deleting a draft revision preserves the live listing and shared images. Audit evidence survives deletion; unreferenced media enters the existing cleanup queue.

`draftListings` is an independent notification preference available to users and dealers, defaulting to true for existing and new accounts. Post-commit email uses existing dealer correspondence routing, opt-out, branded layout, environment delivery guards and a stable delivery key. A delivery failure reports that the drafts were saved. No email is sent during automated tests.
