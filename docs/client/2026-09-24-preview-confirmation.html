FAQ

A public FAQ is available on preview at itrader.im/faq. It is linked in the footer between Buyer Safety and Contact, included in the sitemap, and remains reachable while the launch holding page is on. Contact, Pricing, and Dealers also link to it.

It has 10 categories and 38 questions: About iTrader, Buying a Vehicle, Selling Privately, Vehicle Listings, Payments, Featured Listings & Refunds, Dealers, Accounts, Vehicle Check, Safety & Moderation, and Support. The answers are written for buyers, private sellers, and dealers. Prices are not repeated there; they point to the pricing page. Listing length, photo limits, and dealer listing caps come from the product settings. Search works on the page, and each question has a stable link. There is no separate content system.

The page title is “Frequently Asked Questions – Buying & Selling Vehicles on the Isle of Man”. Its canonical address is https://itrader.im/faq, and the page can be indexed. Breadcrumb information is included. FAQ rich-result markup was not added, because Google stopped showing that search treatment from 7 May 2026. A Q&A markup type was not used either, because Google reserves that for a page about one question where users can submit answers.

Which documents changed

The public legal pages are served from the current policy documents. There is not a second, older set on the site.

The policies were aligned across several updates in August 2026: the full set was first published on 15 August, safety and moderation wording was updated on 17 August, and punctuation was normalised on 22 August. That August work did not remove the nine differences below. The earlier Terms still quoted fixed prices, included trailers and parts, required an Isle of Man location, and said a dealer could cancel in account settings.

Immediately before last night, those four documents did not share one version. Terms and Dealer Terms were version 2026-08-17.2. Private Seller Terms was version 2026-08-17.1. The Refund Policy was version 2026-08-14.1. The “before” wording below is the production wording from that point. Last night changed only these four documents, now version 2026-09-23.1, effective 23 September 2026:

  • Terms and Conditions
  • Private Seller Terms
  • Dealer Terms
  • Refund Policy

Privacy Policy (version 2026-08-17.2), Cookie Policy (version 2026-08-17.1), Acceptable Use Policy (version 2026-08-17.2), and Vehicle Check Terms (version 2026-08-17.1) were not changed. Acceptable Use already prohibits parts and number plates unless iTrader later permits them. The £100 liability floor in Terms clause 17.3 was not changed.

These nine choices were selected during development so the public wording matches how the site already behaves. They are not a separate written approval from you. Please confirm them before they are promoted from preview to production. No payment-provider cancellation, review-time promise, or new vehicle category was built.

If the site is later switched on to require acceptance of the current documents, a signed-in person may be asked to accept the relevant account, listing, or dealer bundle again when they reach an action that requires it. That check is currently off, so visitors are not all being prompted now.

Decisions

1. Prices

The legal documents now follow the price displayed at checkout, including a current launch offer. Listing caps remain 30 for Dealer Starter, 100 for Dealer Pro, and 60 days for a private listing.

2. What can be advertised

The public categories were already cars, vans, motorbikes, and motorhomes. Terms now uses that same scope.

3. Where a vehicle can be located

iTrader remains an Isle of Man marketplace. A vehicle may be located in the Isle of Man or the United Kingdom, which is what search and the sell form already allowed. The pricing page and the FAQ answer “Is iTrader only for the Isle of Man?” now say the same thing.

4. Editing a live advert

Submitting an edit keeps the last approved advert online until the change is checked. iTrader may still unpublish it during review. The same replacement was made in Private Seller Terms, section 4, and Dealer Terms, clause 8.2.

5. Review timing

The pricing cards said “Moderation within 1-2 days” and “Priority moderation”. The page shown after a listing is submitted said the team would check it within 1-2 business days. Nothing in the product enforces that timing or a dealer priority queue, and no service promise was built.

Pricing cards now say “Listings are checked before or after publication”. “Priority moderation” was removed from both Starter and Pro. The submission page now says iTrader checks listings before or after publication and does not promise a review time.

6. Cancelling a dealer subscription

The site cannot cancel the payment-provider subscription itself. A dealer emails hello@itrader.im and may also use the dashboard request where that option is shown. Staff complete the provider cancellation. It takes effect at the end of the paid period, with no pro-rata refund. The dashboard request appears only when that option is switched on; it is currently off.

7. Buyer messages

Anyone can browse a listing. Sending a message through Contact Seller now requires a signed-in account, both on the page and before the message is checked or sent. The FAQ already said sign-in was required.

8. Dealer accounts and private listings

Sign-up and account wording said private selling stayed available on the same account. The site already rejects a private listing from a dealer account, and a dealer listing from a private account. That separation was kept. A dealer account now says it advertises through dealer listings, and the private-seller launch offer is no longer shown on the dealer sign-up path. Becoming a dealer moves the account onto dealer listings.

9. Dealer dashboard

Pricing presented the dealer dashboard as a Pro feature. Access already allows a Starter or Pro dealer with an active entitlement. The marketing now lists the dealer dashboard on both plans. Access was not restricted to Pro.

Also completed

Dealer onboarding, already on production

Invitation links now open on the site that sent them. Sign-in recovery, the return to onboarding, and saving a completed onboarding were fixed. An invitation sent before that correction cannot be reused and must be sent again. Onboarding emails show the full message rather than collapsing it, and they use the iTrader logo. Complimentary Pro access starts when the invitation is accepted and runs through 31 December 2026, so a launch date is not required.

On preview, the onboarding list follows the selected preview packs and a matched account that can be claimed. Four preview test accounts are available for that testing. On production, the five real dealer accounts remain. A temporary production test account used for the checks was removed afterwards.

Admin and email, on preview only

Admin lists for users, dealers, listings, regions, categories, the waiting list, and media now use one compact Actions menu. Payment, report, review, cancellation, and other specialist screens were left as they were. Outbound emails share one branded design. Account permissions, review rules, sign-in links, cancellation handling, and email delivery were not changed. No database migration was run, and this work was not sent as a live email campaign.
